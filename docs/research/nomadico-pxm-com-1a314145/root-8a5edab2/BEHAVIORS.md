# Observed Behaviors

- Header is fixed. At page top it has an orange promotion banner and inset white rounded nav. After scrolling, the promotion banner disappears and the nav becomes full-width.
- Hero uses a looping muted MP4 with dark overlay and white copy anchored near the lower-left.
- Vehicles, experiences, and journal use horizontal sliders with visible next/previous controls. Cards snap to the rail.
- Benefits are static, with four orange circular illustrations connected by a dashed line on desktop.
- Experiences section switches to dark green with white editorial copy and image-led cards.
- Testimonials are a review carousel with image, five-star rating, quote, author/date, and Google Reviews CTA.
- About section uses oversized moving-style marquee typography above a two-column image/copy block.
- Instagram section is a warm yellow image grid. Footer uses a pale blue field, large beach image CTA, newsletter, and dark green link bar.
- Mobile behavior: navigation collapses to menu button, grids stack, horizontal rails remain swipeable, decorative connector simplifies, and hero type scales down.
- Reduced motion: autoplay hero video is hidden and CSS transitions/scroll behavior are disabled.
